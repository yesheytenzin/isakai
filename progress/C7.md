## Delivery review details

Restore default-confined isolation proof for the gate without authoring a replacement fixture: per [N1](N1.md) remove the harness dependency on the deleted RuntimeProbe.java using existing runtime/application diagnostics and observable isolation state, prove the gate fails for real isolation errors, then rerun all three workflows using default per-app UID/confinement, signed APK policy and clean installs/upgrades.

**Additional acceptance / stop gate:** Three useful flows, persistence, >30 seconds, exit zero, no leaked children/UIDs; changed signer rejected; rollback leaves prior app/data usable. Any isolation bypass fails release.

**Original planning estimate:** 3–7 days once app flows and runner exist.


## Acceptance

All three apps complete their defined flows with expected saved results, remain usable beyond 30 seconds, and exit cleanly. Upgrade/rollback preserves data and a changed signer is rejected.

## App-specific acceptance

- **OpenCalc:** Enter a calculation, verify the result, copy it, resize, close cleanly.
- **Simple Calculator:** Calculate, navigate settings and back, change an applicable preference, restart and check persistence.
- **Simple Notes:** Create and edit a Unicode note, save, close, reopen, verify contents and navigate back from editing.
- Every app must remain usable beyond 30 seconds and pass under default confinement.
- Screenshots must accompany asserted actions and saved results; a window screenshot alone is insufficient.
- Substitute a candidate only with a recorded reason; do not replace a failing app merely to turn the gate green.
